About Me

I am a PhD student in the LAST-JD Programme "Law, Science and Technology" at the University of Bologna, Department of Legal Studies (IUS/20 Philosophy of Law), supervised by antonino.rotolo. I am currently on exchange at the University of Luxembourg for my second year.

Before my PhD, I graduated with a Juris Master in Computational Law from Tsinghua University. I also worked as an IT consultant and served as a United Nations volunteer for UNAIDS.

My research interests span legal technology, LLM fairness and robustness, AI ethics, and comparative law between China and the EU (including AI law, data trading law, and autonomous driving law). I am a feminist researcher committed to reducing the gender gap in technology and strengthening international legal governance of emerging technologies.
